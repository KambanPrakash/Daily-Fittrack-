Daily 100g Budget Protein Guide

High-efficiency sources under ₹80–₹100/day

Target: 100g
Soya Chunks (Raw 50g) ~26g Protein

Cost: ~₹6–₹8. Highest protein-to-cost ratio in India. Boil, squeeze water twice, sauté with spices.

3 Whole Eggs ~19g Protein

Cost: ~₹20–₹22. Complete amino acid profile with healthy fats and choline.

1 Cup Thick Dal (Cooked) ~9g Protein

Cost: ~₹10–₹12. Moong or masoor dal paired with minimal rice or roti.

Curd / Dahi (150g) ~6g Protein

Cost: ~₹12. Probiotics support digestion and steady metabolic health.

Roasted Chana (35g snack) ~8g Protein

Cost: ~₹7–₹8. Crunchy, high-fiber snack that keeps hunger at bay.

Toned Milk (250 ml) ~8g Protein

Cost: ~₹14–₹16. Perfect warm bedtime drink to aid overnight recovery.

Non-Negotiable Rules For 65 kg

  • Strict Oil Discipline: 1 tablespoon of oil has ~120 empty calories. Stick to 2–3 teaspoons total per day.
  • Eat Protein First: Always consume your eggs, soya, or curd before finishing your rice or rotis.
  • Liquid Calorie Ban: No fruit juices, sodas, or sweet tea. Drink water, plain lemon water, or black coffee.
